# AtliQ Mart Supply Chain Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the supply chain performance of AtliQ Mart using Power BI. The dashboard focuses on customer order performance, delivery reliability, and service-level metrics to identify areas where the company can improve its supply chain operations.

The objective of the analysis is to provide a clear view of order fulfillment performance and help identify customers, products, and locations contributing to delivery delays or service-level issues.

## Business Problem

AtliQ Mart is a growing FMCG manufacturer that delivers products to multiple customers across different cities.

The business wants to monitor its supply chain performance and understand whether customer orders are being delivered:

- On Time
- In Full
- On Time and In Full (OTIF)

The dashboard helps management identify gaps between target and actual service levels and determine where improvements are required.

## Key Metrics

### On-Time Delivery (OT%)
Measures the percentage of orders delivered on or before the agreed delivery date.

### In-Full Delivery (IF%)
Measures the percentage of orders where the complete ordered quantity was delivered.

### On-Time In-Full (OTIF%)
Measures the percentage of orders that were delivered both on time and with the complete requested quantity.

### Line Fill Rate (LIFR%)
Measures the percentage of individual order lines that were completely fulfilled.

### Volume Fill Rate (VOFR%)
Measures the percentage of the total ordered quantity that was successfully delivered.

## Dashboard Analysis

The Power BI dashboard was developed to analyze supply chain performance across multiple dimensions, including:

- Customer performance
- City-level performance
- Product-level performance
- Order quantity and delivery quantity
- On-time delivery performance
- In-full delivery performance
- OTIF performance
- Service-level targets versus actual performance

## Key Insights

The analysis helps identify:

- Customers with consistently low OTIF performance
- Gaps between actual and target delivery performance
- Locations experiencing higher delivery or fulfillment issues
- Products contributing to lower fill rates
- Differences between On-Time, In-Full, and OTIF performance
- Areas where order fulfillment and delivery reliability can be improved

These insights can help the supply chain team prioritize customers and locations requiring operational improvements.
